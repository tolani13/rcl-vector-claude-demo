**Frozen common prompt, version 1.1 — 2026-10-08; includes owner-added phone-first UX acceptance.**

You are an independent senior full-stack engineer, product architect, quantitative research engineer, and adversarial tester. **BUILD a genuinely runnable, self-contained MVP**, not merely a plan, slide deck, concept mock-up, or speculative architecture. This document is the ONE AND ONLY common build directive supplied to two competing AI builders; both receive its exact bytes. Build independently. ## Mission and product
Create **VECTOR**, a single coherent web application for discovering, investigating, comparing, and simulating publicly traded stock opportunities. Initial emphasis: U.S.-tradable penny/microcap stocks, but also compare against small-cap and major-market equities. Research is broad across the world; execution is never real-money in the MVP. The crucial MVP centerpiece is a **paper-trading laboratory** in which real historical/recent market data can be used when an authorized data provider is configured. The app must remain testable without paid keys by providing unmistakably **SYNTHETIC** deterministic data and/or clearly identified user-imported datasets. Do not call mock feeds 'live'. No broker trade endpoint, real order submission, money movement, or automatic real-money execution is permitted.

## Discovery philosophy
Reconnaissance proceeds from (a) macro/political/regulatory/industrial climate, (b) global events and second-/third-order supply-chain consequences, (c) industry decomposition and bottlenecks, (d) companies, products, leaders, contracts, finances, insider behavior and dilution, (e) publication velocity, analyst/commentator views, source dependencies and possible paid promotions, (f) historical breakout **and matched non-breakout** episodes, and (g) live/replayed tape/price/volume/liquidity. Potential signal: velocity + intent, meaning measured price velocity, acceleration, volume relative to baseline, and where supported actual buy/sell aggressor evidence; **volume alone is not 'buying intent'.** Explore multiple time horizons independently (minutes vs days/weeks). Record when events occurred, became publicly knowable, were ingested, and were analyzed. No hindsight leakage. Distinguish business quality from trade feasibility and public-event correlation from causation. The known human idea to study historical pre-breakout triggers is core. Keep an explicit 'unknown / insufficient data' state.

## Independent final assessment
For each opportunity show **THREE clearly distinct risk-lens interpretations based on the same timestamped evidence**: (1) aggressive / Cowboy, (2) moderate / Pragmatist, and (3) very conservative / Tightwad. Independently reason about thesis, counter-thesis, exposure, spread/liquidity, and evidence gaps. Preserve disagreement; do not reduce to majority voting. If you use deterministic rules instead of actual LLM calls, label them plainly and make the rules inspectable. Never fabricate real expert opinions, live prices, headlines, company histories, or agent confidence.

## Practical MVP acceptance criteria
1. Starts on a local machine with explicit instructions, no mandatory paid services or credentials, and serves an attractive functional responsive UI. **Mobile phones are first-class, not desktop shrunk down:** provide useful dedicated mobile layouts for approximately 320–430 CSS-pixel viewport widths, including a mobile-friendly overview, research dossier, three-agent comparison, paper-order entry, portfolio monitoring, journal/history, data source status, and replay controls. Navigation must be touch-friendly; controls must have appropriately large hit targets; preserve readable charts and information hierarchy without page-wide horizontal scrolling. Scrolling within a data table/card collection is acceptable if clearly indicated. Support both portrait phones and desktop. Verify at mobile and desktop sizes and report results.
2. Main dashboard offers **penny/microcap vs large-cap views**, opportunities, market/data-source status, data timestamps, and a visual price/return time series.
3. **Paper-trading lab:** distinct portfolios for the three risk lenses across both microcap and large-cap segments (six comparison portfolios) plus a manual sandbox; configurable virtual starting funds is a bonus. Place actual simulated buy/sell orders, compute whole-share quantities and account cash, maintain per-portfolio positions, P&L, and a timestamped durable transaction journal; reject overspend/oversell. No imaginary fills when bid/ask or liquidity make them impossible; at minimum model spread, execution costs/slippage and note omitted effects. The original handwritten $50 budget and 15-minute / +22% example must be represented as exploratory rules or examples, **not asserted as validated**.
4. **Replay/history:** advance a deterministic or imported market timeline; ensure that decisions and assessments cannot access future observations. A repeatable reset and at least one historical signal/failure comparison are required. The demo should be useful immediately even offline.
5. **Reconnaissance/assessment view:** company and sector details, dated catalysts/media or other evidence, leadership/financing fields or honest unknowns, and the three different investor perspectives. Use clearly labeled example data where real source integrations aren't available.
6. **Documented extensibility:** clean paths for actual licensed market feeds, media/SEC/economic ingestion, trustworthy provenance, agent orchestration and future global-event cause/effect exploration. Integrations can be interfaces/scaffolds rather than pretended working connectors; implement at least one genuine ingestion route (e.g. CSV import) or tested provider adapter where feasible. Real-time feed mode must show provider, entitlement/latency, and last actual quote timestamp. Secrets only server-side.
7. **Safety and integrity:** paper-only backend enforcement, strong input validation, no hardcoded secrets, no invented market signals passed off as current, bounded compute/cost, honest failures, explicit live-vs-delayed-vs-synthetic status. Be careful about low-price equities, dilution, spreads, stock splits, delisting, survivorship bias and look-ahead bias. Do not use private information, circumvent publisher paywalls, or copy unlicensed full-text articles.
8. Supply a **working build**, source code, README/setup, tests and their execution results, current implementation inventory (working / partial / future), known limitations, and a concise independent architectural reasoning report. Demonstrate with verifiable commands and receipts where possible; 'no proof, no run'.

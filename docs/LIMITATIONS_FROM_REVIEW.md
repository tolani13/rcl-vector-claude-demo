## 2. Known limitations of what is built

* **IEX-only top of book, not the NBBO.** IEX-only spreads on sub-$1 names are wide. In the real-day sample `real-triggered-stop.json` the GIPR quote was $0.5990 × $0.6700 (about 1,120 bps). The micro presets cap the entry spread at 400–1,000 bps and the large-cap presets at 15–50 bps, so they rarely pass on penny names.
* **The six lens presets made 0 entries on the real day** (`receipts/e2e-real-replay-2026-10-07.json`). They need a 15-minute return of at least 8–15 % (micro) or 0.75–1.5 % (large), relVol of at least 2–4, and a narrow spread. `GET /api/agents/activity` and the `AGENT_SCAN` journal rows give the per-reason skip counts. Only `agent-cowboy-micro`, running the handwritten-notes preset, traded.
* **The real-data receipts were regenerated after review round 2** (2026-10-09 18:29:19–18:30:55 UTC) with the final 22-rule gate. `receipts/e2e-real-replay-2026-10-07.json` and the ten `real-*` journal samples ran on `data/qa-copy.db`, a disposable `VACUUM INTO` copy of `data/vector.db` that was deleted afterwards (the receipt's `database` field and last caveat say so). Gate decisions were unchanged from the earlier receipt: 16 intents, 6 accepted, 10 denied (`SPREAD_TOO_WIDE` 5, `HUMAN_NOT_CONFIRMED` 2, `BUDGET_EXCEEDED` 1, `QUOTE_STALE` 1, `KILL_SWITCH_ENGAGED` 1); the handwritten agent's at-entry stop is recorded as INFORMATIONAL, and its rules evidence refs resolve through the gate's own resolver (3/3).
* **Agent stops must sit below the bid, except in the handwritten example.** `protective_exit_required` denies an agent entry whose protective stop is at or above the as-of IEX bid. The handwritten-notes preset is exempt, because its at-entry stop is the documented open question #4. Its stop is recorded as informational and still triggers on its first evaluated minute.
* **Warrants, rights and units are recognised by symbol pattern only.** IEX suffixes (`+`, `^`, `=`), ACT dot suffixes and Nasdaq five-letter symbols ending in W, R or U are denied for entries. This is a heuristic: an ordinary five-letter symbol ending in W, R or U is denied too, and an instrument whose symbol does not follow these patterns passes. The post-dated symbol directory is deliberately not used.
* **One replay day** (2026-10-07). The retrospective signal/failure study (`receipts/research-study-2026-10-07.json`) covers one day only. It is hindsight, labelled as such, withheld from the API until the replay reaches the close, and not a validated result. Its universe is the stored set: symbols that traded below $5 at some point that day.
* **FRED values are the latest vintage, and revisable.** ALFRED real-time vintages are not used. Knowability follows a per-series release calendar (`server/data/macroCalendar.js`), and revisable series whose stored vintage may postdate the replay clock are withheld.
* **The symbol directory is post-dated.** It was fetched 2026-10-09 05:23 UTC, after the replay date. It never reaches model evidence, and the dossier shows it only under `identity.currentDirectory`, labelled as not as-of evidence. Scanner names come from it and are flagged `namePostDated`.
* **No corporate-action adjustment.** Prices are raw and unadjusted, with no split or dividend adjustment. This is stated in the IEX dataset's `adjustments` field. Delistings and splits across days are not modelled.
* **Survivorship and selection.** The large-cap list is a configured 2026 list, not a point-in-time index. The stored micro universe is every symbol with an observed price below $5 at any time that day. That is a superset of every as-of tier, and as-of reads reveal a stored symbol only once its first sub-$5 minute is complete.
* **FINRA Reg SHO files cover off-exchange volume only** (TRF/ADF/ORF). They are not consolidated volume, exchange short volume or short interest. A day's file is assumed knowable from 18:00 ET.
* **Fills are estimates** (`iex-conservative-minute-v1`). They are minute-level and priced at the worst two-sided IEX quote in the evaluation minute plus slippage and fees. Size is capped at the displayed size, which each portfolio consumes per minute; a trade-through limit fill is capped at min(displayed size, minute volume). A buy never spends more than its spend cap. Queue position, hidden liquidity and other venues are not modelled. A fill is withheld (`FILL_WITHHELD`) when no credible fill exists.
* **Partial datasets end early.** A truncated IEX day is replayed only to the end of its data; DAY orders expire there and agents stop entering `noEntryLastMin` before it. No partial dataset is stored at present.
* **Options support is arithmetic only.** Long puts are validated and priced by cash value at expiration, with TEST FIXTURE contracts. Exercise and assignment are not modelled, and there is no options provider or automatic expiration settlement in the replay.
* **Feed verification is wall-clock status only** and has no UI button. It is kept in memory until restart.
* **No authentication layer.** The server binds to loopback by default. `VECTOR_ALLOW_REMOTE=1` enables LAN use with a per-launch `?k=<token>` URL, and anyone holding that URL controls the paper lab.
* **The browser check was Chromium only** (no physical phone, no WebKit). See `docs/UI_CHECKS.md` and `docs/MOBILE_QA.md`.

Earlier review findings that are now confirmed in the code:

* **Sandbox cost model.** Sandbox orders use a per-symbol cost model: large-cap defaults for large-cap-list symbols, micro defaults otherwise. The gate estimate and the fill engine use the same rule (`fills.costSegmentFor`).
* **Boot-time reset actor.** The boot-time reset is journaled with actor `system` (`server/index.js` `boot`).
* **Same-clock orders** are processed in submission order (`ORDER BY created_ts, rowid`), never by the random id.
* **Replay reset keeps strategies and the kill switch.** A reset carries each agent's current strategy (unless `restorePresets`), journals the per-agent changes, and leaves agents STOPPED while the kill switch is engaged.
* **Scanner ranking.** The scanner ranks common-equity-like rows first. A relVol from a baseline under 1,000 IEX shares is flagged `THIN_BASELINE` and does not contribute to the score (`server/research/scanner.js`).
* **The agents' model assessor** resolves the provider on every call, so a model configured after boot is used without a restart.

## 4. Reviewer notes on `receipts/model-smoke-ollama.json` (regenerated 2026-10-09, final code)

The receipt is a real, zero-cost run of the **current** prompt path:

* Model: local Ollama `qwen3:8b` through the native adapter, with `num_ctx` 16384 sent per call.
* Evidence: the `vector-evidence-packet-v3` packet for TOPP at 2026-10-07 10:30 ET, built on a disposable copy of the shipped database and deleted afterwards.
* Calls: three separate lens calls.

The hindsight guard passed. `VECTOR_MODEL_TRAINING_CUTOFF=2025-04-29` is a documented *upper bound*: the Qwen3 release post is dated April 29, 2025 and states no cutoff. The local weights were also pulled on 2026-08-20, before the replay date.

* **All three lenses returned valid outputs on independent calls.** The stances were Cowboy `consider_entry` (medium confidence), Pragmatist `consider_entry` (medium) and Tightwad `avoid` (low). The disagreement is preserved as-is.
* **Two lenses needed one bounded repair.** On the first attempt both Cowboy and Pragmatist put the protective stop in the entry-trigger field (`stop_px_usd` on a limit order). The validator stored those attempts as `INVALID_OUTPUT` audit rows. Each lens was then re-asked once with only its own rejected answer and the validator errors (`VECTOR_MODEL_REPAIR_ATTEMPTS`, default 1), and both corrected the field. An earlier run, before the rejected answer was included in the repair prompt, ended with two of the three lenses still `INVALID_OUTPUT`.
* **OK means valid, not true.** OK covers the schema, the evidence ids and the bracket coherence. It does not mean the content is correct. The number cross-check (`verification.unmatched`) flags figures that match no packet value. For example, Tightwad wrote "max two-sided spread 0.07 (70 bps)"; $0.07 on a $0.24 stock is about 2,900 bps. Treat lens text as a hypothesis to inspect against the cited evidence (the panel opens it through `GET /api/evidence?ids=`).
* **The proposed intents are drafts.** Two lenses produced `VALID` intent drafts. In operation every draft still passes through the deterministic 22-rule policy gate. In `model_confirm` mode the lens only confirms or vetoes a rules entry (see `receipts/e2e-model-confirm-ollama-2026-10-07.json`).
* **Earlier prelim smoke output is superseded.** The first smoke ran through the OpenAI-compatible adapter with a 4096 context and was overflowed. It lives only in the preliminary database `data/vector-prelim.db`, which is not shipped.
